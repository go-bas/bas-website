Questions

Will callers know they’re talking to AI?

We recommend being upfront about it, and in some situations disclosure is required. Most callers care far more about getting a fast, helpful answer than about who gives it.

Do I need a new phone number?

No. We can forward your existing number to the AI receptionist all the time, only after hours, or only when your team doesn’t pick up.

What if it doesn’t know the answer?

It takes a message or transfers the call to a person, based on rules you choose. It never makes up an answer.

How long does setup take?

It depends on how many call types you have and which systems it connects to. We’ll give you a timeline after the free assessment.
