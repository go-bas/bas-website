Contract & document review

Know what you’re signing before you sign it.

AI reads your contracts, leases and vendor agreements, highlights the clauses that matter, flags what’s unusual or missing, and pulls out every date and dollar amount — so your attorney’s time goes to the parts that need it.

What it handles

Plain-English summaries

What the document says, who owes what, and how to get out of it.

Risky clause flags

Auto-renewals, liability, indemnity, exclusivity and payment terms that deserve a second look.

Missing terms

Compares against what you normally expect and points out what isn’t there.

Key dates and amounts

Renewal deadlines, notice periods and fees pulled into one list — with reminders.

Version comparison

Shows exactly what changed between the draft you sent and the one that came back.

Your own playbook

We teach it your standard positions, so its flags match how your business works.

AI-assisted review, not legal advice. This service helps you and your attorney work faster. It does not replace a licensed attorney, and important agreements should still be reviewed by one.

Questions

What kinds of documents can it review?

Contracts, NDAs, leases, vendor and service agreements, purchase orders and similar business documents.

Is my information kept private?

Yes. Documents are processed only for your review, are not used to train public AI models, and are handled under a written confidentiality agreement.

Can my attorney use it?

Absolutely — many teams send the AI summary and flags to their attorney along with the document, which shortens the review.
