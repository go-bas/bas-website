Questions

Is this cold calling?

No. We focus on people who already reached out to you or are existing customers — the calls that convert best and keep you on the right side of calling rules.

Can it send texts too?

Yes. Calls can be paired with text follow-ups, such as a reminder link or a booking link after the call.

Which CRM does it work with?

Our own CRM, or the one you already use. We’ll confirm the connection during the assessment.
