Questions

Do I need QuickBooks or Xero?

Yes, we work inside QuickBooks Online or Xero. If you use neither, we will help you set one up.

Do you do taxes?

No. We keep your books ready for tax time and work with your CPA, or refer you to our partner CPA. We don’t prepare or file tax returns.

How many transactions are included?

Up to 300 a month across 2 accounts. Busier businesses add $99 for each extra 150.

Who sees my financial data?

Only the people working on your account. You can remove our access at any time from your accounting software.

Bookkeeping, not accounting or tax advice. Tax returns and tax planning are for a licensed CPA.
